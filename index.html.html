Learn & support

Clear, supportive information about cognitive wellbeing and screening.

Explore these topics

🧠

What is cognitive wellbeing?

Memory, attention, language, reasoning and other skills can vary with sleep, stress, mood, health and age.

🩺

Understanding screening tools

Screening can help identify whether further evaluation may be useful. It is not, by itself, a diagnosis.

 
🌤️

Everyday habits that support your mind

Regular sleep, physical activity, social connection, managing long-term health conditions and meaningful activities can support general wellbeing.

💬

When should I talk to a professional?

If memory or thinking changes are persistent, worsening, or affecting daily life, consider discussing them with a qualified healthcare professional. Sudden confusion needs urgent medical attention.

A gentle reminder

One activity can’t tell your whole story.

Everyone forgets things sometimes. If you’re concerned about changes, a healthcare professional can consider your history, symptoms, medicines and other possible causes.

MindCare Assess is a student demonstration project. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
